# Crude-oil desk — primary sources

Use only these hosts. Reach the exact pages below; do not follow links to other hosts.

## EIA (host `www.eia.gov`) — the desk's core

- Weekly Petroleum Status Report: https://www.eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/
- Weekly U.S. crude oil stocks summary: https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_sum_sndw_a_EPC0_SAX_mbbl_w.htm
- Cushing, OK crude stocks (weekly): https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/hist/LeafHandler.ashx?n=PET&s=W_EPC0_SAX_YCUOK_MBBL&f=W
- U.S. crude oil production (monthly): https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_crd_crpdn_adc_mbblpd_m.htm
- Refinery utilization (weekly): https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_pnp_unc_dcu_nus_m.htm
- Crude oil imports (monthly): https://www.eia.gov/dnav/pet/pet_move_impcus_a2_nus_ep00_im0_mbbl_m.htm

## Other primary hosts (available, rarely the lead for crude)

- EPA: https://www.epa.gov/renewable-fuel-standard-program (RFS context for fuel blending).
- CARB: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/our-work/programs/low-carbon-fuel-standard (California fuels context).
- Federal Register: https://www.federalregister.gov/ (rule actions affecting crude/petroleum).
- USDA AMS: https://www.ams.usda.gov/market-news (rarely needed for crude).
- USDA ERS: https://www.ers.usda.gov/ (rarely needed for crude).

# Biofuels desk — release calendar

Times are U.S. Eastern unless noted; all releases land on the hosts in `sources.md`.

## Weekly

- **EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report** (ethanol production and stocks) — Wednesdays at 10:30 a.m. ET (Thursday at
  11:00 a.m. when Monday was a federal holiday). `eia.gov/petroleum/supply/weekly/`.
- **CARB LCFS credit transfer activity report** (weekly LCFS credit price and volume) — published weekly, typically
  Tuesday, on `ww2.arb.ca.gov`.
- **USDA AMS National Weekly Ethanol Report** — weekly spot ethanol and co-product prices on `ams.usda.gov`.

## Monthly

- **EPA EMTS RIN generation** — monthly RIN generation by category on `epa.gov` (EMTS).
- **USDA ERS Oil Crops Outlook / Feed Outlook** — monthly feedstock supply-and-use on `ers.usda.gov`.

## As announced

- **EPA RVO proposals and final rules** — the annual renewable volume obligation rulemaking, in the Federal Register
  (`federalregister.gov`) when proposed and final.
- **CARB LCFS amendments** — benchmark and program changes on `ww2.arb.ca.gov`.
